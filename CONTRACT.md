## 1. Team Procedures

- We will communicate primarily through our team WhatsApp/Discord group and GitHub, and will check in regularly so everyone stays up to date on progress.
- We will hold a short team meeting (in person or online) at least once a week to discuss progress, blockers, and next steps.
- Tasks will be divided as fairly as possible based on each member's strengths, availability, and interest, and will be tracked using a shared task list or GitHub Issues/Projects board.
- All code will be pushed to the shared GitHub repository regularly, with clear commit messages, so that everyone's contribution is visible.
- Major decisions (e.g. architecture choices, changes to scope) will be discussed and agreed on as a team before being implemented.
- If a member is unable to complete a task or attend a meeting, they will notify the team as early as possible so plans can be adjusted.

## 2. Team Expectations (Review and Discuss)

- Every member is expected to contribute meaningfully and consistently to the project, not just at the end before deadlines.
- Members are expected to be honest about their progress, ask for help when stuck, and offer help to teammates who are struggling.
- We expect open, respectful communication, and that disagreements will be raised and worked through as a team rather than left unresolved.
- Work should be completed to a reasonable standard and in line with the module brief and rubric, so that no one member has to redo another's work.
- Deadlines set by the team (which may be earlier than the official submission date, to allow time for review) are expected to be respected.
- This list is not fixed — the team will revisit and adjust these expectations together if circumstances change.

## 3. Consequences for Failing to Follow Procedures and Fulfil Expectations

- If a member is not meeting expectations, the team will first raise it directly and informally, to understand what's going on and see how the team can support them.
- If the issue continues, it will be discussed openly in a team meeting, and a plan with clear, agreed next steps will be put in place.
- If problems persist despite these efforts, the team may escalate the matter to the module lecturer/facilitator for guidance or mediation.
- Any escalation will be a last resort, used only after genuine attempts have been made to resolve the issue within the team.
- The intention of this section is not to punish, but to protect the team's ability to deliver good work together and to be fair to all members.
